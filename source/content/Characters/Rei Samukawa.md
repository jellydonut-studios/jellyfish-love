**Rei Samukawa** (寒川黄) is an Egg Yolk jellyfish, and a former student at the [[Crown Academy]]. He was the keyboardist and former leader of [[HYPERDOLPHIN]].

# Physical Description
---
As a jellyfish, Rei had abnormally long tentacles that would often break off. When drawn, he has a star on the side of his bell.

As a human, Rei has choppy hair that is orange on the top and white on the bottom. His eyebrows are round, and he often wears an orange windbreaker.
# Personality
---
Though Rei was rather clumsy and accident-prone, he was a blindingly upbeat jellyfish, inspiring those around him to follow their passions. He latches onto the idea of "love", often chasing after a poor jellyfish in hopes of snatching a date.

# Etymology
---
_Samukawa_ is written as 寒川:
- "寒": Cold
- "川": River
	- He was named for the Japanese name of the jellyfish, サムクラゲ (*Samu Kurage*)- translating to cold jellyfish.

_Rei_ is written as 黄:
- "黄": Yellow
	- This is referring to the yellow colouration of the jellyfish.
