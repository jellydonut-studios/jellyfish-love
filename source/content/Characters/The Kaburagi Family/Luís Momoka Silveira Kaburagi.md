**Luís Momoka Silveira Kaburagi** (冠城シルベーラ百嘉ルーイース) is a Crown Jellyfish and a 1st year at the [[Crown Academy]]. 

# Physical Description
---
As a jellyfish, Luís is a typical Crown Jellyfish of his species. He is on the smaller side of an average jellyfish height.
Unlike most Crown Jellyfish in Japan, Luís has golden yellow gonads- a fact which he is very proud of. 

As a human, Luís is one of the smallest characters in the cast, and looks the youngest as well. His hair is light blue, and he is often drawn with a small, yellow crown. 
Like much of his family, he has spectral heterochromia, with brown eyes and a small slice of yellow.

# Etymology
---
Luis is Brazilian-Japanese, so he has multiple names in accordance to Portuguese naming traditions.

_Kaburagi_ is written as 冠城:
- 冠: Crown
- 城: Castle
- Named for the "Crown Jellyfish" (Coronatae), which is the clade that Ephyra Jellyfish are a part of.

# Fun Facts
---
- Luis was once referred to as:
	- Momoka Ameku (百嘉天久)
		- *Ameku* (天久) having the characters for Heaven (天) and Long Time (久)
			- 天 referred to his royal lineage
			- 久 referred to the longevity of the jellyfish's looks ()
		- *Momoka* (百嘉) having the characters for 100 (百) and praise (嘉)
		- I eventually changed this name because it didn't feel particularly inspired, nor did it encapsulate the jellyfish very well
	- 
- He is named after Fábio Lang da Silveira, one of the two researchers who initially described his species
	- I was told by a Brazilian friend that Silveira is not a very realistic surname, so I may change it to Silva at some point. I'm still on the fence about it though.
