The Kaburagi Family (冠城家) is a clan of Ephyra Jellyfish (*Nausithoe punctata*) in Underwater Japan with its history spreading back all the way to the old ages, taking immense pride in the fact that they are one of the oldest Jellyfish species alive. 


# History
---
The Kaburagi Family established the [[Crown Academy]] nearly 200 years ago.

# Etymology
---
*Kaburagi* is written as 冠城:
- 冠: Crown
- 城: Castle
- Named for the "Crown Jellyfish" (Coronatae), which is the clade that Ephyra Jellyfish are a part of.
