**Achlys Ortega** (オルテガ・アクリス) is a Black Sea Nettle and a 1st year at the [[Crown Academy]].

# Physical Description
---
As a jellyfish, he is large and a deep violet colour. Not too distinct from other jellyfish of his species.

As a human, he is incredibly tall and sports a dual toned hair style, with one side covering his eye. 
He also sports a scar from the bottom of his chin. Unlike most students, he wears his uniform with the sleeves rolled up.
# Personality
---
Unsurprisingly, Achlys is cold and standoffish, though he does have a good heart underneath all that.
He is incredibly self-conscious of the fact he is larger and darker than other jellyfish his age, and despises being singled out for his looks.

# Etymology
---
_Ortega_ is written as オルテガ:
- Ortega refers to the Spanish last name, meaning "Nettle" (a direct reference to the name of the jellyfish)

_Achlys_ is written as アクリス:
- Achlys is a direct reference to the species name for the Black Sea Nettle, which refers to the Greek Goddess of pain and misery.
- It's just really cool.
