# Personality
---
Sui is about as normal of a jellyfish as anyone can get, being a mild-mannered moon jellyfish.
However, thanks to his adoptive parents, he grew up with a profound love of collecting trash and has a hobby of using it for crafts.
# Story
---
Sui was adopted by two comb jellyfish, who happen to work at Trash Collectors in [[Underwater Kyoto]].
