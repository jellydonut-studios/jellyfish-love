**Kiseki Ishiboshi** (石星きせき) is a Cauliflower jellyfish and a 4th year at the [[Crown Academy]]. She is the lead vocalist and secondary guitarist for [[HYPERDOLPHIN]].

# Physical Description
---
As a jellyfish, Kiseki is a normal Cauliflower jellyfish who is noted to have a better maintained complexion than most other jellyfish. 

As a human, Kiseki has spikey long, purple hair going down to shoulder length. Her eyes are emerald green.

# Etymology
---
_Ishiboshi_ is written as 石星
- "石": Stone
- "星": Star
	- When put together, the word "ibo" is present in-between, representing the Japanese name of the jellyfish, イボクラゲ (*Ibo Kurage*), meaning "Wart Jellyfish"
	- Star also refers to her career in the spotlight

_Kiseki_ is written as きせき:
- "きせき": Miracle
	- Kiseki comes from 奇跡 (*Kiseki*), meaning "miracle"
