The **Nozaki Family** (野崎家) is a clan of Ghost Jellyfish (*Cyanea nozakii*) who own a shrine in Underwater Kyoto. They excel in exorcisms, and have a stronger spiritual connection than most jellyfish do.

# Etymology
---
_Nozaki_ is written as 野崎:
- 野: Town, Village
- 崎: Cape
- Named from the scientific name of the Ghost jellyfish (*Cyanea nozakii*). 
	- Unfortunately I cannot translate the original document describing this jellyfish, so I can't find out who this jellyfish was named after. Instead, I opted to use the most common combination for it.
