**Eko Hiue** (火上衣子) is the third daughter and sixth eldest child of [[The Hiue Family]].

# Physical Description
---
As a jellyfish, Eko looks to be an average *Morbakka virulenta*, though one of her eyes have scarred and are dysfunctional. 

As a human, Eko is tall-ish girl with long black hair, tied up in twin tails. She often wears an eye patch on her left eye, which is scarred.
As with all the members of the Hiue family, she also has two tufts of hair on the top of her head resembling horns. 
She is often seen wearing elaborate outfits, though hides weapons underneath them.

# Personality
---

As with many of her siblings, Eko is a ruthless individual who rarely shows her emotions. She was very perceptive, and often looked for weak points even before approaching people. 
She wasn't a jellyfish of many words, and was very blunt.

She despises ugly things, and thus takes great pains to make her surroundings as nice and orderly as possible. 

# Story
---

Born into the Hiue family, Eko was forced to participate in the brutal tradition that was to kill her siblings, or be killed herself. She adapted to her surroundings surprisingly well, though she disliked how messy family business often was.

To cope, Eko often imagined herself being born a *Cyanea* jellyfish, untethered by her surroundings and frilly- her preferred form.
