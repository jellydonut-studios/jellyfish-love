# Physical Description
---

As a jellyfish, Gorou has a relatively average build for a *Morbakka virulenta*. Unlike most of his siblings, however, he doesn't have any major physical blemishes on his body.

As a human, Gorou is an anxious-looking person with tan skin and large, nervous eyes. Similarly to his jellyfish forms, his body has no major physical blemishes, and he notably has central heterochromia (with a majority of his eyes being black, with a lavender band of colour around his pupils).
