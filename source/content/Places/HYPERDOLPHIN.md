**HYPERDOLPHIN** (ハイパードルフィン) is a high school band associated with the [[Crown Academy]]. Currently, the leader is [[Kanzou Sugama]].
# History
--- 
HYPERDOLPHIN was started by [[Kiseki Ishiboshi]] in her first year with [[Rei Samukawa]].
