**The Shinigami that is also a Jellyfish** is a game set in the [[index|Jellyfish Love!]] Universe, featuring the main character [[Shinki Sakasai]] and the titular [[Shinigami kun|Shinigami kun]], her partner in crime.

It is the spiritual spin-off of [[The Jellyfish Otome Game]], mainly featuring the families of characters that appear in there but has no real impact on the game itself.

# Fun Facts
---
- This game is largely inspired by Kenishi Yonezu's "[Shinigami](https://youtu.be/8nxaZ69ElEc?si=RULp5xu-0gAOVI4y)"
	- A rough design for Shinigami kun came first, along with a few other concept sketches for a main character. 
		- A *Periphylla periphylla* was considered, but I chose to make the main character a *Cassiopea ornata* for a couple of reasons
			- 1) I felt like having a jellyfish that needed light to live would fit the character much better than a jellyfish who took damage from it
			- 2) I felt that the existing [[The Sakasai Family|Sakasai Family]] could fit into the story rather well, instead of making a new character and fitting them into the story
- I hope to also make an art book to go along with the game. I think it'd be real neat to show my process in creating the characters and have someplace to put my neat little doodles. 
- Unless I get a translator for this game, it probably will only be available in English.
